## 3. Key Findings on 2FA & Privacy Stance
1. **SMS OTP Rejection Validated**: The decision to omit SMS OTP in favor of pure RFC 6238 Authenticator App TOTP is strongly validated. Artisans operate inside shielded steel-roof workshops where mobile signal is often weak, and they distrust recurring SMS telecommunication fees.
2. **Zero-Margin Leakage Guarantee**: Artisans universally praised the local disk encryption of wholesale rates (`wholesale_rate_per_kg`). Competitor workshops cannot extract cost structures.
3. **Cross-Platform Accessibility**: The combination of Tauri v2 desktop executable, Flutter 3 mobile companion, and universal web app ensures full operational coverage from the office to the cutting saw.
